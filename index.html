Why power backup

What a single power cut can cost you

Harsh power supply and sudden failure hurt productivity, equipment and data. A proper backup system is cheaper than the downtime.

01

Data loss

Sudden power failure can wipe unsaved work and corrupt critical data.

02

Lost man-hours

Idle teams and stopped machines turn outages into direct productivity loss.

03

Unsafe costly equipment

Surges and unstable voltage shorten the life of expensive machinery and electronics.

04

Meetings & entertainment

Lose news, Zoom meetings, entertainment and domestic appliances the moment power drops.

05

Unstable voltage

Critical instruments need stabilized voltage — a stabilizer is not optional.

06

Go lithium

Lithium Ferro Phosphate batteries mean no maintenance and a small footprint.
