Inverter questions

What is the difference between an inverter and a UPS?

A UPS is designed for sensitive loads such as computers and servers and switches to battery with minimal delay. An inverter suits homes, shops and lifts where a short switch-over is acceptable. See our UPS page if you have critical equipment.

Which inverter do I need for my lift?

Choose our lift inverter, a heavy-duty static inverter / lift UPS built so elevators keep moving during outages. Share the lift motor rating and building details in the enquiry form and we will size it.

Do you provide service and AMC?

Yes. We provide Annual Maintenance Contracts for all our products and have a service network across Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Delhi / UP and Jharkhand.
