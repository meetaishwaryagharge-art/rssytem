About RS Systems & Solutions LLP

Generic power equipment with branded quality. Customized power quality products for small organizations and big industries since 2020.

A quality-conscious Indian company on a single platform

RS Systems & Solutions LLP is a CE, ISO 9001:2015 and NABL certified Indian company dealing in customized power quality products that meet our clients’ standards. We offer a full range of power conditioning products and services to reduce the blow of today’s harsh power supply and power failure.

Established in 2020, we are a young company with more than 30 years of experience behind it. Our backbone is Mr. N. S. Ramsing (Malustay), a professional with 32 years in the power quality industry. Under his leadership we deliver quality products, keep to delivery timelines, provide dependable after-sales service and keep adopting updated technology at a competitive cost.

We are also dealers for branded UPS & inverters, and we provide Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for all our products. Our key objective is products that are relevant, affordable, easy to maintain and true value for money.
