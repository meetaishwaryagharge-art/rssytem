Solar questions

Should I choose on-grid or off-grid?

Choose on-grid to reduce electricity bills where the grid is stable. Choose off-grid with MPPT where the grid is unreliable or absent, or where you need solar power with battery storage. Tell us your location and load and we will advise.

Which batteries do you supply?

We supply SMF, tubular, solar and lithium batteries. Lithium Ferro Phosphate batteries need no maintenance and have a small footprint.

Do you provide service and AMC?

Yes. We provide Annual Maintenance Contracts for all our products and have a service network across Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Delhi / UP and Jharkhand.
